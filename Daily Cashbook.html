Quick Help Guide

        
      

      

        
🚀 Getting Started

        
Use the tabs to navigate between Daily Entry, Bank Entry, and Cash Count. Ensure you select the
          correct Date before entering data.


        
🔒 Verification & Locking

        
Once you finish counting physical cash in the Cash Count tab, click "Verify & Lock". This
          locks the day to prevent accidental changes. Admins can unlock records if needed.


        
📊 Reports & Exports

        
Go to the Reports tab (Admin only) to view history and charts. You can export data to
          Excel, PDF, or copy a WhatsApp-ready summary to your clipboard.


        
☁️ Cloud Sync

        
If the status says "Cloud", your data is syncing with Google Sheets. If "Local", it's stored in
          your browser only.
